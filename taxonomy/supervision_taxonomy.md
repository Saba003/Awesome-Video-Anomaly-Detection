# Supervision Taxonomy

- **Supervised:** Frame-, segment-, or video-level labels for normal and anomalous samples.
- **Semi-supervised:** Training mainly or exclusively on normal data.
- **Weakly supervised:** Coarse labels, usually at video level.
- **Self-supervised:** Proxy tasks generate supervisory signals from unlabeled videos.
- **Unsupervised:** No manually assigned labels.
- **Training-free:** Uses pretrained foundation models without task-specific training.
