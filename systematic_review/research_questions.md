# Research Questions

- **RQ1:** Which supervision paradigms dominate deep learning-based VAD?
- **RQ2:** How have architectures evolved from CNN/RNN systems to transformers and foundation models?
- **RQ3:** Which datasets and metrics are most frequently used, and what biases do they introduce?
- **RQ4:** How well do current methods address latency, false alarms, scalability, and edge deployment?
- **RQ5:** How is explainability evaluated in VAD?
- **RQ6:** Which research gaps remain for open-world, multimodal, privacy-aware, and training-free VAD?
