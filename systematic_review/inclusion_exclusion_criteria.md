# Inclusion and Exclusion Criteria

## Include

- Video-based anomaly or abnormal-event detection
- Deep learning, foundation-model, or hybrid methods
- Peer-reviewed journal and conference papers
- High-relevance preprints for emerging paradigms
- Sufficient methodological or evaluation detail

## Exclude

- Image-only anomaly detection
- Non-video time-series anomaly detection
- Papers without accessible full text
- Duplicate or extended versions without meaningful additional contribution
- Pure action recognition without an anomaly-detection objective
