# Model Selection Guide

## Only normal training data

Consider memory-augmented autoencoders, teacher-student models, one-class objectives, or pretrained feature-distance methods.

## Video-level anomaly labels

Consider MIL-based architectures, weakly supervised transformers, or vision-language models.

## Real-time edge deployment

Prefer lightweight CNNs, compressed temporal models, windowed attention, quantization, and small input resolutions.

## Explanations required

Combine spatial and temporal attribution with object-level evidence or natural-language reasoning.

## Open-vocabulary anomalies

Consider vision-language and multimodal foundation models, but evaluate prompt sensitivity and domain bias.
